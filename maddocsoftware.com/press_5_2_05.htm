MAD DOC® SOFTWARE UNVEILS EMPIRE EARTH® II “THE NEW KING OF REAL-TIME STRATEGY GAMING”

With PC Gamer and IGN.com Editor’s Choice nods, Empire Earth® II ships to retail stores with critical acclaim. PC Gamer Magazine declares: “Empire Earth® II is the
New King of Real-Time Strategy Gaming.”

BOSTON, MA – MAY 2, 2005 – Mad Doc® Software, LLC (Mad Doc), an emerging force in game development, is proud to announce that Empire Earth® II, developed by Mad Doc for Vivendi Universal Games (VU Games), has shipped to retail stores in the US and is available for purchase. The game carries a suggested retail price of $49.99 for the PC and has been rated “T” for Teen audiences by the ESRB.

Fans can expect a new age of epic conquest in Empire Earth II. Already winning accolades from gaming publications, Empire Earth II has garnered the coveted Editor’s Choice award from PC Gamer, receiving an incredible 94% review score from the world's best-selling PC game magazine. Their review, to be published in the June 2005 issue, proclaims: "Empire Earth II is the new king of real-time strategy," and adds: “This is rip-roaring strategy for gamers that love the RTS genre and have been waiting for it to get on with growing up.”

Empire Earth II’s great reception doesn’t end there. IGN.com, one of the Internet's largest entertainment news destinations, has awarded Empire Earth II their prestigious Editor's Choice award, hailing the game as a "truly deep and enjoyable real-time strategy experience on all levels.” Audiences abroad agree: Spain’s Micromania Magazine gave Empire Earth II “Game of the Month” honors, with review scores of 95 and 96 for single and multiplayer.

“Our team has put their heart and soul into this game, and through countless hours of the development cycle, we’ve had one clear goal in mind: to make the absolute best RTS game experience possible, to deliver a truly exceptional gameplay experience for fans,” notes Dr. Ian Davis, Mad Doc CEO and Creative Director on Empire Earth II. “We’re thrilled that the game is being so well received. It’s just wonderful to have the game publications recognize the tremendous effort we’ve put in over the last few years and the innovation we’ve brought to the table with this one.”

Building upon the success of its predecessor (GameSpy's 2001 PC Game of the Year), Empire Earth II delivers on its promise to be an “Epic. Conquest. Evolved.” Mad Doc has added a host of innovative features; smart, core streamlining features that make the gameplay experience quite exceptional. Pushing the envelope, the team has delivered non-cheating, cutting-edge AI, and with innovative features like the Diplomacy System, the Crown System, Territories, the Citizen Manager, the Picture-In-Picture, and the War Planner – fans can expect this game to deliver hundreds of hours of fresh gameplay.

“I’ve been saying all along that once fans get their hands on this game, once they really wrap themselves in what we’ve done here, they’ll be blown away by all that’s going on in the game,” Dr. Davis continues. “That day is here, and I’m just going to sit back and watch the fun unfold.”
