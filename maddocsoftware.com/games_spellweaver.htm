Once again at the forefront of gameplay advancement, Mad Doc® Software, LLC (Mad Doc), today unveiled SpellWeaver™, a groundbreaking role playing game for the PC pushing the envelope in gaming with patent-pending MadAI™ Character AI and RPG UI Speech Recognition technologies that create uniquely human character personalities affected by the way you play, set amidst a fully immersive gaming experience in which players use spoken word to cast spells and engage in multi-player magic duels online.

Currently in pre-production, SpellWeaver continues the tradition of Mad Doc’s trademark polished AI, merging the best elements of classic RPGs with intense action and uniquely human AI behavior, served up in a fresh new experience that takes gameplay and immersion to a whole new level.
