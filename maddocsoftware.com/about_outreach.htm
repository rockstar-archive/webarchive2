Mad doc & Education: A Winning Combination

Mad Doc has a history rich in education and outreach. Since our inception, we’ve worked hard to entertain, educate and inspire young minds. Whether opening our doors for tours, tutoring, or teaching at such prestigious universities as MIT and CMU, we’re spreading the good word on games and the value of a great education. Beloved partners, for years, Mad Doc has spoken at Merrimack College and supported the Lawrence Boys & Girls Club close to our headquarters. Through donations, mentoring and visits to our hub, we do our best to lend a hand in our own small way (our CEO has even been spotted donning a Santa suit).
