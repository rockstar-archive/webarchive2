The development team behind such critically acclaimed hits as Empire Earth® II, Dungeon Siege®: Legends of Aranna™, and Star Trek® Armada II, Mad Doc® Software, LLC has been spearheading the creation of Triple A titles for years. Mad Doc was founded in 1999 by Dr. Ian Davis with the goal of developing major titles that expand the game playing experience and broaden the market through creative use of new Networking, Graphics, and AI technologies (Empire Earth® II uses Mad Doc’s Mad3D™ Game Engine and MadAI™ Character AI technologies). An emerging force in the computer games industry, Mad Doc consists of industry veterans with experience in every aspect of game development and an unmatched expertise in Artificial Intelligence. A registered Xbox®, Xbox 360™ and PlayStation®3 developer with a proven record of strong game design and on-time, on-budget delivery, Mad Doc team members are collectively responsible for over 20 million units in the game market worldwide. Our latest titles have kept our tradition of excellence alive and well. VUG’s Empire Earth® II garnered a coveted PC Gamer Editor’s Choice Award and was named an Academy of Interactive Arts & Sciences Strategy Game of the Year Finalist, while Bethesda Softworks® Star Trek® Legacy has been hailed as “The Best Space Combat in Ages.” Currently, the team has several titles in production.
