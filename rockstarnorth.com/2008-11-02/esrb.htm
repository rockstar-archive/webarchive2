What information does Take Two Interactive collect?

Take Two Interactive only collects personal information from you on a voluntary basis. When you submit personal information to Take Two Interactive, it will usually take the form of: (1) product registration; (2) newsletters; (3) sweepstakes; (4) tech support; (5) message boards; (5) shopping at our online store; (6) online submission of resumes for job openings; or (7) obtaining investor information. The information we collect may include personal information such as your name, date of birth, e-mail address, phone number, and home address. In addition, we may collect demographic information such as your age, gender, favorite styles of gaming and the systems you own or plan to buy.

* Product Registration. If you wish to register your product online, you will be asked to provide us with personal information such as your first and last name, home address, telephone number and email address. We also ask you to enter information about the product you are registering such as the game's title and platform.
* Newsletters. If you wish to receive newsletters, you will be asked to provide us with personal information such as your first and last name, home address, and email address.
* Tech Support. If you want to submit a question or problem to our technical support center using Take Two Interactive's email form, you will be asked to provide your name and email address, along with a description of your problem.
* Message Boards. Whenever you give out personal information in public areas such as on a message board or in chat room, that information can be collected and used by people you don't know. While we strive to protect those who wish to participate in these forums, we cannot guarantee the security of any information you post in these areas.
* Online Store. If you purchase products or merchandise through our online store, you will be required to provide additional information to complete your order, including a valid credit card number with expiration date and billing address.
* Resumes. Take Two Interactive allows for the online submission of resumes. Personal information collected from resumes will be used solely for the purpose of accepting and evaluating candidate submissions for job postings, and is therefore not governed by this Privacy Policy. Information obtained from resumes may be shared within Take Two Interactive.
* Investor Relations. Take Two Interactive provides investor relations materials on its websites. Personal information collected on the investor relations' portion of the Take Two Interactive website is used solely in a business capacity and is not governed by this Privacy Policy.

Whatever the activity may be, we will only collect information to the extent deemed reasonably necessary to fulfill your requests and our legitimate business objectives.

What happens if I refuse to provide my personal information?

Your refusal to submit personal information may limit your ability to participate in some of our activities such as sweepstakes or process your online product registration. However, you do not need to provide personal information to Take Two Interactive in order to use our websites.

Does Take Two Interactive use cookies, track IP Addresses or use other passive means to collect information?

Take Two Interactive uses cookies on its website. A "cookie" is a small bit of record-keeping information that websites often store on a user's computer. Cookies do not include people's names, e-mail addresses, or other information that is personally identifiable; they are typically used to quickly identify a user's computer and to "remember" things about the user's visit. Take Two Interactive also keep tracks of Internet Protocol (IP) addresses for safety and security reasons. An IP address is a number that is used by computers on the network to identify your computer every time you log on to the Internet.
