Rockstar North, one of the world’s leading video game developers, is a thriving creative community of developers from a variety of backgrounds and nationalities. We are based out of modern, spacious, purpose-built studios in the heart of Edinburgh . We develop original game titles and are proud to be the developer of the phenomenally successful Grand Theft Auto series of games. Recent titles are Grand Theft Auto; San Andreas for Playstation 2, Xbox & PC and in partnership with Rockstar Leeds, Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories for PSP. At Rockstar North we work closely with our publishing partner Rockstar Games and have been part of the Rockstar family of developers since 1999. Rockstar North is a wholly owned subsidiary company of Take Two Interactive Software Inc.
