Animator

Team Bondi is an independent game development company based in Sydney, Australia. Headed up by Brendan McNamara and featuring a team of seasoned games professionals, Team Bondi will produce next-generation console titles.We are offering a quality salary and lifestyle package to the right individuals.We are looking for a talented 3D Animator with experience in both keyframed and motion capture pipelines to join our Team to work on the upcoming next-generation console title L.A. Noire.This position is on contract for a period of 4 - 6 months.

Required Experience

  • Proven ability in at least one game development production seen through to completion
  • Thorough understanding of animation pipelines within games development
  • A very good eye for detail and quality
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to work in a creative and collaborative environment
  • A passion for creating and playing video games
  • Commitment to personal and peer development and sharing best practices

Required Qualification

  • Extensive knowledge of both keyframe and mocap production pipelines
  • Ability to animate realistic characters and bring performances to life
  • Demonstrate a thorough knowledge of all the principles of animation
  • A thorough understanding of weight and timing in realistic motion contexts
  • Experience in cutscene work a bonus
  • Extensive knowledge of both Maya and Motion Builder

If you are a strong Junior, and would like to be considered for a junior position, please also drop us a line.

If you want to be a part of this challenging undertaking, email us at animation.jobs@teambondi.com
