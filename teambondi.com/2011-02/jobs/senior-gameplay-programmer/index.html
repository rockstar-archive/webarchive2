Senior Gameplay Programmer

Team Bondi is an independent game development company based in Sydney, Australia. Headed up by Brendan McNamara and featuring a team of seasoned games professionals, Team Bondi will produce next-generation console titles.

We are offering a quality salary and lifestyle package to the right individuals.

We are looking for a talented programmer to join our Gameplay team, using technology to create next-gen entertainment through player control, world interaction, mission creation, and story integration.

Required Experience

  • At least one shipped title as Senior Programmer
  • At least 3 years games industry experience
  • Extensive collaboration with designers

Required Skills

  • Excellent C++
  • Strong architecture, documentation and design skills
  • Strong technical leadership and mentoring skills
  • Game mathematics, physics and simulation background an advantage
  • Advanced animation sequencing and blending an advantage
  • Pathfinding, AI and NPC behaviours experience an advantage
If you want to be a part of this challenging undertaking, email us at code.jobs@teambondi.com
