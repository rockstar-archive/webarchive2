Senior Game Designer

Team Bondi is an independent game development company based in Sydney, Australia. Headed up by Brendan McNamara and featuring a team of seasoned games professionals, Team Bondi will produce next-generation console titles.

We are offering a quality salary and lifestyle package to the right individuals.

We are looking for an enthusiastic and talented Senior Game Designer on a design-led, high profile next-generation console title. Team Bondi offers excellent opportunities for those wishing to work in a team atmosphere with a staff of respected veteran engineers on an original IP project. Successful candidates will report directly to the Lead Designer.

Required Experience

  • Minimum 4 years design experience
  • Credited Senior Designer, having shipped at least 2 AAA, X-Box or PS2 Action/Adventure titles
  • Team management experience
  • Experience with 3D software preferably with Maya
  • Experience of sandbox style game-play
  • Have a sound knowledge of AI constraints and design fundamentals

Required Qualifications

  • A bachelor of arts degree in arts or product design or have overwhelming game design experience
  • Understanding of basic programming or scripting languages is a plus

Required Skills

  • Excellent oral and written communication skills
  • Able to focus and work without micro-management
  • Ability to take direction and accept critical review of your work
  • Clear understanding and appreciation of schedules and milestones
  • A passion for and broad knowledge of console games with an ability to critically analyse them
If you want to be a part of this challenging undertaking, email us at design.jobs@teambondi.com
