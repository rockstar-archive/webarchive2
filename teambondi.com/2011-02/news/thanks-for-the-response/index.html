L.A. Noire is our attempt to recreate a 1940’s detective thriller as a game. Crime thrillers are a huge genre in both literature and film, so in theory there is a large potential audience.

A detective thriller, as a game, relies on the player being able to tell when a suspect is lying. This requires new technologies that can deliver real performances and human nuance. This is not an easy row to furrow but we are enjoying the challenge.

We still need more help so if you’re a big Chandler, Hammett or Ellroy fan and still love making video games please get in touch.

We hope that you are interested in what we are attempting and we would like to thank you for the very encouraging response we have had so far.
