BUILD ENGINEER
We are seeking a highly talented Build Engineer to develop and maintain the export, build, and automatic test pipeline for our new next-gen title.   Responsibilities
  • Develop and maintain scripts that take exported assets from 3DS Max through our processing tools and into the game.
  • Develop and maintain scripts to automatically generate builds of all of our tools and engines for PC, PS3 and Xbox 360, reporting any errors back to the team.
  • Develop and maintain scripts to run automated tests of our tools and game and report any errors back to the team.
  • Develop and maintain scripts to automatically generate final images of the game for each target platform and deliver them for QA.
  • Ensure that all relevant files from the build processes are correctly held in our Perforce depot.
  • Build scripts should work with a distributed build system where applicable.
  • Assist the team in making the best use of Perforce so that no one is unnecessarily waiting for files and bad checkins are kept to a minimum.
  • Some work on tools may also be required (e.g. 3DS Max exporters, various editors and pipeline tools).
  Required
  • At least 2 years experience working on build systems.  Previous experience in a games development environment is desirable, but not required.
  • Experience working with SCM tools (training in Perforce can be given if necessary).
  • Experience of scripting tools (Python preferred).
  • Ability to work with non-technical members of the team to help them solve problems.

NETWORK PROGRAMMER

If chucking packets around a network and a deep interest in online / multiplayer games are your thing then you could be just the person we are looking for.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain and extend the development of our current game networking systems on Xbox360 and PS3.
  • Work with other programmers to interface with all project subsystems, including character control, AI, and physics.
  • Work with external vendors to develop solutions using and/or extending their online service SDKs.
  • Define programming policies and guidelines to aid other programmers in creating networking-compatible software.
  • Develop and maintain software to aid testing of networked games.
  • Continue development of networking system to be compatible with all features of shared game framework, including abstracting online service user interface APIs.
  • Co-write technical design and software design documentation

Required

  • At least 6 years of programming experience with experience in network programming.
  • Advanced programming skills (C++ and possibly other languages).
  • Technical problem solving ability.
  • Ability to work within a schedule.
  • Ability to work well as part of a creative team.
  • Self-motivated with a strong work ethic.
  • Excellent oral and written communication skills.
  • Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering or Computer Science, or equivalent work experience.

Desired

  • Broad understanding of computer and game networking systems, including DNS, NAT, firewall, security methods, peer-to-peer and client server models.
  • Experience with game console networking, including Wii, Xbox Live and Sony PS2.
  • Experience on Xbox360 and/or PS3.

SENIOR TOOLS PROGRAMMER
We are seeking a highly talented Senior Tools Programmer to head up a small team of programmers to design, develop and maintain the exporters and editors our team will use to develop our new next-gen title.   Responsibilities
  • Assist the Lead Programmer in recruiting and managing other tools programmers on the team.
  • Working with the art and design leads, design, develop and maintain the tools that will help the designers and artists to add content to the game quickly and efficiently.
  • Maintain our 3DS Max export tools (experience with COLLADA would be helpful).
  • Ensure that procedures are in place to test all tools before they are released.
  Required
  • At least 5 years experience designing and writing tools for non-technical end users.  Previous experience in a games development environment is desirable, but not required.
  • Excellent programming skills (C#/C++ and .NET experience preferred).
  • Experience of scripting tools (Python preferred).
  • Good knowledge of 3D maths.
  • Ability to work well as part of a creative team.
  • Ability to work within a schedule.
